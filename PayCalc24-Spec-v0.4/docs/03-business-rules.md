# PayCalc24 — Business Rules Summary
Key rule families:
- BR-GEN: Company isolation, effective date, immutable history.
- BR-ORG: effective payroll assignment/history.
- BR-FUND: formula/allocation/carry-forward/coverage.
- BR-COMP: dynamic component execution by scheme and sequence.
- BR-ATT: import, dynamic attendance policy, derived inputs.
- BR-KPI: weighted achievement and Gate after achievement.
- BR-P3: dynamic Floor/Target/Maximum, interpolation, Eligible vs Paid, Coverage.
- BR-GROSS/NET: dynamic Gross, statutory provider results, Net Pay and employer cost.
- BR-INPUT: input lineage and audited override.
- BR-CALC: deterministic snapshots and formula versions.
- BR-SIM: isolated what-if calculation.
- BR-PERIOD: Draft→Inputs→Validated→Calculated→Reviewed→Approved→Locked→Exported.
- BR-REPORT/AUDIT: dynamic reports, snapshots, explainability and immutable audit trail.
