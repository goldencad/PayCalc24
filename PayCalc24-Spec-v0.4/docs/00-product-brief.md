# PayCalc24 — Product Brief
PayCalc24 is a configurable Payroll, Performance & Compensation platform.

## Scope
- Company-scoped payroll using TS24 Core for User/Company/Permission.
- Reuse employee master through `CompanyId + EmployeeCode`.
- Dynamic payroll funds, pay components, compensation schemes, attendance, KPI/performance, inputs, formulas and reports.
- Calculate compensation, P3/variable pay and Gross Pay.
- Integrate with iBHXH/TaxOnline or other providers for statutory results and Net Pay.
- Support simulation/back-test, approval, locking, audit and explainable payroll.

## Principles
Configuration-driven, policy-driven, versioned, effective-dated, explainable, deterministic, integration-neutral and multi-language from first release.
